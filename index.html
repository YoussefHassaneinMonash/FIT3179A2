15 Seasons, 18 Clubs: What Really Wins?

Since Greater Western Sydney joined in 2012, the AFL has had 18 clubs. Every one of them has played every season since. That makes the last 15 years a fair contest. So who has dominated, does playing at home really help, and what do winning teams do differently?

Footy terms in 30 seconds

Ladder
The league table. Teams are ranked by wins (4 points each, 2 for a draw).
Percentage
Points scored ÷ points conceded × 100. It's the tie-breaker when teams have equal wins.
Finals
The knockout series after the regular season, played by the top 8 (top 10 since 2026).
Premiership / flag
Winning the Grand Final, the last game of the season.
Inside 50
Moving the ball into your attacking zone, the 50-metre arc in front of goal.
Clearance
Winning the ball out of a stoppage (a bounce or throw-in) and sending it forward.
Contested possession
Winning the ball while under physical pressure from an opponent.
Margin
How many points the winning team won by.
Chapter 01

Who ruled the era?

Three clubs won three flags each: Hawthorn, Richmond and the Brisbane Lions. But winning premierships and being consistently good are not the same thing.

Geelong: never far from the top

Geelong won only one flag (2022). Yet they have the best average ladder finish of any club (4.3), and they played finals in 13 of the 15 seasons.

Gold Coast, the newest club, has played finals only once.

Pick a club from the menu to trace its journey up and down the ladder.

Consistency beats a hot streak

Geelong and Sydney top the win tally by a clear margin. Both have also been to plenty of Grand Finals but won just one each. Over 15 years, steady seasons add up to more wins than a short golden era.

Chapter 02

The geography of footy

Ten of the 18 clubs are Victorian, but the game is played from Perth to the Gold Coast, and even in Darwin, Alice Springs, Shanghai and Wellington.

Footy-mad states

In 2026, Victorians bought about 67 tickets for every 100 residents. South Australians were close behind at 64, helped by the league's Gather Round in Adelaide.

In New South Wales and Queensland, the rugby league states, it was fewer than 10.

Chapter 03

Does home ground matter?

Every club wins more often at home, but the size of that advantage varies a lot.

The Kardinia Park fortress

Geelong won 82% of its home games. Its compact ground suits its own players and is unfamiliar to visitors.

Collingwood has almost no home advantage. Most of its "away" games are also at the MCG, in front of its own fans.

Distance is a hidden opponent

A typical West Australian club travels about 59,000 km a season. That's more than three times the 18,000 km of a typical Victorian club. Each dot is one club in one season.

Rivalries and close calls

Chapter 06

2026: a three-peat, and who's rising

Brisbane beat Fremantle by seven points to win a third straight flag, matching Hawthorn's 2013–15 run. Behind them, the ladder had a major shake-up.
